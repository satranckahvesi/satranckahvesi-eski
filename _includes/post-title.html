{%- comment -%} A post title, broken into two lines after its first ": " (colon and space): the part up to and including the colon is a block-level .title-lead set at half the size of the rest. The colon and the space after it stay in the text (copying, screen readers) but are not shown (.title-colon). title: the title {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign title_parts = include.title | split: ": " -%} {%- if title_parts.size > 1 -%} {{ title_parts[0] }}: {{ include.title | remove_first: title_parts[0] | remove_first: ": " -}} {%- else -%} {{- include.title -}} {%- endif -%}
